The foundry approach

A disciplined route from market requirement to reusable product, with evidence at every important gate.

One product system

Shape. Forge. Prove. Graduate.

The sequence is simple on purpose. Each stage answers a different question before the product earns more investment.

01 / SHAPE

Should this exist?

Define the client requirement, user, constraint and accountable business result.

02 / FORGE

What should repeat?

Build the reusable core and the tools that make it useful across engagements.

03 / PROVE

Can we trust the loop?

Test actions, observations, evidence, cost and operating behaviour under controlled conditions.

04 / GRADUATE

Can it stand alone?

Prepare the product, channel, governance and ownership needed for independent growth.
